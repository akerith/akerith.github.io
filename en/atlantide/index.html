Puzzle game for Android

AtlantideGates of Light

Beneath the sea, Atlantis sleeps. Its gates open only to light. Rotate crystals, push stones, ride the currents and split beams with prisms: bring light to every gate and wake the city, hall by hall.

Available on Google Play

Moves: 0

What is inside

  • 100 puzzles in 10 halls. Designed one by one. Every hall adds a new idea: lightstreams, tides, prisms, coloured light, twin portals.
  • An endless Tower. Every floor is a little harder than the last, never unfair.
  • A new puzzle every day. Easy on Monday, tough on Sunday.
  • A city reborn. Every hall you complete lights up a district of Atlantis and reveals a piece of its story.
  • Your own pace. Undo and restart whenever you like. If you are stuck, a hint shows you the right move.
  • Made for your phone. Play one-handed, offline too (reward videos need an internet connection). In six languages.

Money and ads in Atlantide

Every level can be solved without paying. There is no energy and there are no lives. These are the rules in force today; if they change, we will write it here.

  • No full-screen ad appears in the first 10 levels.
  • After that, at least two and a half minutes and at least three levels pass between one ad and the next.
  • No more than five appear in one play session.
  • In the Tower an ad may appear every five floors, starting from floor 11.
  • Reward videos play only when you choose them: none starts by itself.

What you can buy: removing the ads, coin and hint packs, the piggy bank and looks, which change colours and give no advantage. No loot boxes. Prices are on Google Play.

These are the studio’s rules, applied to this game.
