Our rules

They hold for every LSGames Studio game, with no exceptions. They are not only written here: they sit in each game’s project, and whoever works on it has to follow them.

  1. Every game can be finished without paying.

    Every level has a solution that needs no purchase. We do not trust our memory on this: automated tests check it.

  2. No energy that runs out.

    Play when you like, for as long as you like. No lives to wait for, no refills to buy.

  3. No fake urgency.

    No made-up countdown to rush your decision. No “today only”.

  4. No paid loot boxes.

    When you buy something, you know what you get before you pay.

  5. No nagging.

    We do not keep interrupting you to offer a purchase.

  6. There are ads: few, and under written rules.

    We do not say “no ads”, because there are ads: they are one of the two ways the studio earns. Each game has fixed rules on when an ad may appear, written on its page with the numbers. Reward videos play only when you choose them.

    The ad rules in Atlantide

  7. We tell you how things are.

    The games are made by one person with AI tools, and each game’s credits say so. When we get something wrong we say it, and we say when it is fixed. Anyone who writes to us gets a real answer.

    How we work

What you can buy

Only optional things: removing the ads (you pay once), coins, hints and looks. Looks change colours and give no advantage. The exact list is on each game’s page; prices are on Google Play.

If a rule is broken

Write to supporto@lsgamesstudio.it and tell us which game and where. We will look at it and answer you.

If one day any of this stopped being true, we would change the game, not the rule.
