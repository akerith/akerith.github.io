Three things that hold for every game we make.

They treat you well

Every game can be finished without paying. No energy bars, no fake countdowns, no paid loot boxes, no nagging. There are ads: few of them, and under written rules.

Our rules

Made well

We care about the details. Before release, every game is tested on a real phone, screen by screen. If something is not good enough yet, it ships when it is.

In the open

The studio is one person, Gaetano, working with AI tools, and we say so. When we get something wrong we say that too, and we give a real answer to anyone who writes to us.

How we work

So how do we earn?

From ads and from optional purchases: removing the ads, coins, hints, looks. You need neither of them to reach the end of a game.

When an ad may appear and what you can buy is written on each game’s page, with the numbers.
