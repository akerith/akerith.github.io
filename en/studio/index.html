One person and an AI.

LSGames Studio is Gaetano, making video games in Italy together with Claude, an AI. This page says who does what.

Who does what

Gaetano decides what to make, chooses, judges and plays. Claude, an AI made by Anthropic, writes the code and carries the technical work. Images, music and film clips come from other AI tools, picked and directed by Gaetano. The choices are his.

Each game’s credits say the same.

What we never claim

We do not write “hand-drawn” about something generated. In Atlantide the 100 hall levels are designed one by one; the Tower floors and the daily puzzle are built by a generator on your phone. We say which is which.

We do not say “no ads”, because there are ads. We do not quote numbers that are not ours and measured.

A different genre every game

The first game is a puzzle game. The next ones will change genre: it is how the studio shows what it can do. The rules stay the same for all of them.

How a game is checked before release

  • Automated tests on the code, including the ones that check every level can be solved without a purchase.
  • Screens compared one by one after every change.
  • A run on a real phone.
  • A group of testers, before release.

Independent

Today the studio has no publisher and no investors. It is one person’s business, registered in Italy, and it earns from ads and from optional purchases inside the games.

Our real accounts

LSGames Studio speaks only from here: this site, the address lsgame.wftd@gmail.com and the @lsgamesstudio profiles on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. Videos come out on YouTube first. A profile with a similar name, anywhere else, is not us. We never ask for passwords, payments or personal data in a comment or a message.

The symbol

A door, a path, a player. The arched door is the way into a game. The path is the road ahead. The orange square is whoever is playing.
